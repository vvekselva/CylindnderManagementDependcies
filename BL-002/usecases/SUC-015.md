## Canonical replacement

Customer delivery now runs through the Add Stop flow:

1. **STORY-0051 — Add Stop Interactive Selection Workflow**: trip/load gate, customer selection, customer-held and vehicle-held cylinder selection, challan context and handoff.
2. **STORY-0085 — Delivery Stop / `POST /stop`**: customer stop transaction and persistence through `CustomerStopSelectionController.processStopIngestion` and `VehicleTripStopIngestionService`.
3. Governed search/challan/photo dependencies remain attached to the active Add Stop use cases rather than this legacy use case.

STORY-0037 and STORY-0038 remain in the repository for audit/history only. They are excluded from approval, post-approval conformance, BL-004, BL-005, BL-009 and BL-011.
